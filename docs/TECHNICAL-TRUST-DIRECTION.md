# Technical Trust: Product and Content Direction

## Purpose

Update TechnicalTrust.org from a newsletter-centered personal site into an integrated platform for:

1. Sales Engineering education and thought leadership
2. A practical Sales Engineering training lab
3. Technical product storytelling and UGC services for B2B technology companies

The site should make the relationship between these three areas immediately clear:

> Technical Trust helps technical professionals and B2B technology companies earn confidence through better discovery, demos, evaluations, and implementation conversations.

## Positioning

Do not present Technical Trust as a generic marketing or UGC brand. The differentiator is the combination of:

- Software engineering experience
- Technical consulting and implementation experience
- Customer-centered communication
- Sales Engineering practice
- Honest, trust-building product education

Recommended short positioning statement:

> Clearer technical conversations. More trustworthy demos. Better buying decisions.

Recommended longer description:

> Technical Trust is a Sales Engineering practice and media platform focused on helping technical professionals and B2B technology companies turn complex products into clear, credible customer conversations.

## Brand architecture

Technical Trust is the parent brand with three connected branches.

### Learn

The weekly newsletter and educational content.

Topics include:

- Discovery
- Demo design
- Technical storytelling
- Buyer trust
- Technical evaluations
- POCs
- RFPs and requirements
- AI in Sales Engineering
- Architecture communication
- Objection handling
- Implementation handoff

### Lab

A public portfolio and training environment where Dan practices and demonstrates Sales Engineering skills.

Each Lab project should show:

- Target persona
- Customer problem
- Discovery questions
- Requirements and constraints
- Proposed solution
- Architecture or workflow diagram
- Demo or working POC
- Limitations and tradeoffs
- Evaluation criteria
- Lessons learned

Projects may use products from partner companies, public tools, or clearly labeled simulated scenarios.

### Studio

The paid service line. Use “technical product storytelling” or “technical demo content” in primary site copy. Use “technical UGC” as a supporting term where helpful, since many B2B buyers may not identify with consumer-style UGC language.

Recommended service description:

> Technical Trust Studio helps B2B SaaS, AI, automation, API, data, and cybersecurity companies explain complex products through credible demos, integration walkthroughs, evaluation guides, and sales enablement content.

## Primary offer

### Technical Trust Demo Sprint

> In one focused sprint, turn a complex B2B product into a clear, buyer-specific demo and evaluation package.

Suggested deliverables:

- Product and stakeholder interview
- Target persona and use-case definition
- Discovery summary
- Demo narrative and script
- Technical walkthrough video
- Integration, workflow, or architecture visual
- Objection and limitation notes
- One-page technical evaluation guide
- Post-publication performance summary, when applicable

Potential future offers:

- Technical POC design and implementation
- Demo environment setup
- RFP and technical-response support
- Sales enablement libraries
- Founder-led demo coaching
- Ongoing technical content retainer

## Content-to-business system

Every newsletter issue should connect to one Sales Engineering competency and one practical artifact.

Example:

| Newsletter idea | Skill practiced | Lab artifact | Client service connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Feature Dump | Relevant demo design | Persona-specific demo | Demo Sprint |
| The Whiteboard Flood | Architecture communication | One-page architecture visual | Technical walkthrough |
| The Vocabulary Test | Discovery and relevance | Discovery question set | Product interview |
| The Happy Path | Honest evaluation | Risks and limitations guide | POC/evaluation content |
| The Eager Yes | Qualification | Success-criteria template | Technical evaluation |

This system should be visible in the CMS/content model where practical. A post may include fields for:

- Sales Engineering competency
- Practical artifact
- Related Lab project
- Related Studio service
- Call to action

## Proof and credibility

Build an evidence log for every Lab or Studio project. Capture:

- Stakeholders interviewed
- Problems and requirements identified
- Solution proposed
- Technical work completed
- Demo or POC delivered
- Limitations disclosed
- Feedback received
- Engagement, leads, calls, or conversion results

Use accurate labels:

- “Client project” for paid or authorized work
- “Portfolio project” for self-directed work
- “Simulated scenario” for fictional or reconstructed work

Do not claim enterprise discovery calls, RFP ownership, or sales POC experience unless the work actually involved those activities.

## Ideal Studio clients

Prioritize B2B technology companies with technically strong products that are difficult to explain:

- Developer tools
- AI platforms and agent tooling
- Workflow automation
- APIs and integration platforms
- Cybersecurity
- Data and analytics platforms
- E-commerce infrastructure
- Enterprise SaaS

## Voice and editorial principles

- Clear over clever
- Specific over generic
- Honest about limitations
- Buyer-centered rather than feature-centered
- Technical without unnecessary jargon
- Practical enough to use immediately
- Trust as an observable behavior, not a vague personality trait

The newsletter can retain its current direct, memorable style, including ideas such as “The Feature Dump,” “The Confidence Bluff,” and “The Whiteboard Flood,” while adding a practical artifact and next action to each issue.

## Success metrics

Track separate metrics for each branch.

### Learn

- Newsletter subscribers
- Open and click rates
- Replies and conversations
- Returning readers

### Lab

- Published projects
- Demo videos completed
- POCs completed
- Portfolio inquiries
- Interview stories generated

### Studio

- Qualified conversations
- Paid pilots
- Revenue per project
- Repeat clients
- Client leads or engagement generated

The near-term goal is not maximum audience size. It is a compounding body of credible work that produces conversations, paid projects, and evidence for Sales Engineering interviews.

## Core strategic idea

Technical Trust should operate as a flywheel:

```text
Newsletter idea
  -> Sales Engineering skill to practice
  -> Demo, evaluation, or POC
  -> Client-facing technical content
  -> Measured result and case study
  -> Stronger portfolio and interview evidence
  -> Better clients and Sales Engineering opportunities
```

Every new site feature, article, and project should strengthen at least one part of this flywheel.
